ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  - 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  - 1/3 cup chopped onion
  - 1/2 cup celery, diced
  - 1/2 cup carrots, diced
  - 1 3/4 cups chicken broth
  - 2/3 cup milk
  - 3 cups cooked chicken, cubed
  - 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  - 1/4 tsp dried thyme
  - Salt and pepper to taste
  - 2 refrigerated pie crusts
steps:
  - Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  - In a saucepan, melt butter over medium heat. Stir in flour, onion, celery, and carrots; cook until vegetables are tender.
  - Gradually stir in chicken broth and milk. Cook, stirring constantly, until mixture boils and thickens.
  - Stir in chicken, frozen vegetables, thyme, salt, and pepper. Remove from heat.
  - Pour filling into bottom pie crust. Cover with top crust; seal edges and cut slits for steam.
  - Bake 30 to 40 minutes or until crust is golden brown and filling is bubbly. Cool 10 minutes before serving.
ingredientsPt:
  - 2 colheres de sopa de manteiga sem sal
  - 1/3 de chávena de farinha de trigo
  - 1/3 de chávena de cebola picada
  - 1/2 chávena de aipo, em cubos
  - 1/2 chávena de cenoura, em cubos
  - 1 3/4 chávenas de caldo de galinha
  - 2/3 de chávena de leite
  - 3 chávenas de frango cozido, em cubos
  - 1 chávena de ervilhas e cenouras congeladas
  - 1/4 de colher de chá de tomilho seco
  - Sal e pimenta a gosto
  - 2 bases de massa quebrada refrigerada
stepsPt:
  - Aquece o forno a 220 °C.
  - Num tacho, derrete a manteiga em lume médio. Junta a farinha, a cebola, o aipo e a cenoura; cozinha até os legumes ficarem tenros.
  - Vai adicionando o caldo de galinha e o leite. Cozinha, mexendo sempre, até a mistura ferver e engrossar.
  - Envolve o frango, os legumes congelados, o tomilho, o sal e a pimenta. Retira do lume.
  - Verte o recheio na base de massa. Cobre com a massa superior, sela as bordas e faz pequenos cortes para o vapor escapar.
  - Assa 30 a 40 minutos, até a massa ficar dourada e o recheio borbulhar. Deixa arrefecer 10 minutos antes de servir.

Every time I make this, I can hear Grandma humming in the kitchen. She taught me that the secret is in the crust — keep it cold, handle it gently, and don't rush the bake. This was the dish that brought the whole family together after church on Sundays, and it still does today.
